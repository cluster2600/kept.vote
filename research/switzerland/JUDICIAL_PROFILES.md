## What their panel actually said

On 19 September 2023, Koch chaired the Second Criminal Law Division panel with
Hurni and Hofmann in **7B_15/2021 / 7B_16/2021**. Section 6.3 says:

> So ist bundesrechtskonform, dass die Vorinstanz die (im Vergleich relativ kurze) Dauer der Vergewaltigung berücksichtigt.

In plain English, the panel accepted the lower court's consideration of the rape's
relatively short duration when assessing the sentence. This is a documented basis
for criticising the panel's sentencing reasoning. It does **not** establish that
each judge personally considered rape unimportant. The published judgment does
not identify the sentence's individual author or each member's vote.

The federal panel also partly upheld the prosecution's appeal and quashed the
cantonal sentence on other grounds. It did not acquit the offender. The distinction
matters: accepting this passage and finding other sentencing errors occurred in
the same judgment. [Original judgment, section 6.3](https://www.bger.ch/ext/eurospider/live/de/php/aza/http/index.php?highlight_docid=aza%3A%2F%2F19-09-2023-7B_15-2021&lang=de&type=show_document&zoom=).

On 18 September 2024, in **6B_612/2024**, the Court expressly disavowed the earlier
formulation as inappropriate. It clarified that short duration must never favour
a rape offender in sentencing; a prolonged assault may aggravate culpability.
That was a separate case, not an order annulling the entire 2023 judgment.
[Court's explanation, published 15 October 2024](https://www.bger.ch/files/live/sites/tfl/files/pdf/de/6b_0612_2024_2024_10_15_T_d_11_14_33.pdf).

Suggested website wording: **“Koch, Hurni and Hofmann sat on the 2023 panel that
accepted consideration of a rape's relatively short duration in sentencing. The
Federal Supreme Court expressly rejected that formulation in 2024.”**

## Sonja Koch

Koch was elected an ordinary federal judge on **25 September 2019**. Her official
biography publishes **UDC/SVP** affiliation. She studied law in Zurich and Lausanne,
joined the Aargau bar in 2004 and completed a doctorate in 2014. Her career includes
work as a lawyer, court clerk, Federal Office of Justice scientific officer and
president of the Bernese Jura-Seeland regional court. She was a deputy federal
judge before becoming an ordinary judge.

Her specific responsibility documented here is **presiding over the 2023 panel**.
That role does not establish sole authorship of its reasoning. The consulted
sources do not establish company board seats.
[Official professional biography](https://www.bger.ch/cms/render/live/fr/sites/tfl/home/federal/federal-richter/federal-richter-bundesrichter/koch-sonja.html).

## Christoph Hurni

Hurni was elected an ordinary federal judge on **23 September 2020**. His official
biography publishes **PVL/GLP** affiliation. He studied in Bern and Bologna, obtained
a doctorate in 2007, joined the Bern bar in 2009 and completed his habilitation in
2017. He worked as a federal court clerk and Bern cantonal judge, including as
president of its Second Civil Chamber. His official biography also lists a titular
professorship at the University of Zurich.

He was a **member of the 2023 criminal panel**. His current listed presidency of
the First Civil Law Division must not be confused with his role in that earlier
criminal case. The consulted sources do not establish company board seats.
[Official professional biography](https://www.bger.ch/cms/render/live/fr/sites/tfl/home/federal/federal-richter/federal-richter-bundesrichter/hurni-christoph.html).

## Yann-Eric Hofmann

Hofmann was elected an ordinary federal judge on **15 March 2023**. His official
biography publishes **Le Centre/Die Mitte** affiliation. He studied at Fribourg,
worked as a clerk at the Federal Administrative Court and Fribourg cantonal court,
and as a lawyer at the Federal Office for Spatial Development. He served as a
deputy and ordinary cantonal judge and a deputy federal judge before his election.

His biography documents professional governance roles: presidency of Fribourg's
eJustice steering committee (2021–2023) and vice-presidency of federal expropriation
commission 5 (2018–2023). These are public professional roles, not evidence of a
company directorship. He was a **member of the 2023 panel**.
[Official professional biography](https://www.bger.ch/cms/render/live/fr/sites/tfl/home/federal/federal-richter/federal-richter-bundesrichter/hofmann-yann-eric.html).

## Additional public governance roles

Hofmann was welcomed as **co-president of the Justitia 4.0 steering committee on
24 February 2025**, alongside Florian Dublin. The project's own announcement
identifies him explicitly as a federal judge. Its current end date has not been
established. [Project announcement](https://www.justitia40.ch/de/news/yann-eric-hofmann-neuer-co-praesident-des-steuerungsausschusses).

The **Swiss association of judges (ASM/SVR)** also lists Hofmann as a **committee
member** on its current public page. This is an association governance role, not
a commercial company directorship. [Committee](https://www.svr-asm.ch/fr/vorstand.htm).
